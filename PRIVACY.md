# EUG Airport: Flights & Guide — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

EUG Airport: Flights & Guide ("the app") is an independent app for travelers using
Eugene Airport (EUG), Mahlon Sweet Field, in Eugene, Oregon. It is published by an
individual developer. It is not an official app of Eugene Airport or the City of Eugene.

This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: **the app does
not collect, store or share any personal information.**

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any
  other personal information.
- It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- It does not sell or share any information with anyone.

## Flight information

To show arrivals and departures, the app requests the public flight list from the
City of Eugene's flight-status service (`coeapps.eugene-or.gov`) over an encrypted
(HTTPS) connection. The request contains only which board to show (arrivals or
departures) and the app's name and version.

As with any internet connection, the city's server can see your device's IP address
when it answers. The developer does not receive, see or keep that information. The
City of Eugene's own privacy practices apply to its server.

The app checks for new flights only while it is open on your screen.

## Settings stored on your phone

Your choices in Settings (appearance, banner, which board to open on, refresh interval,
12- or 24-hour time) and whether you have seen the "What's new" notes are saved on your
phone only. They are never sent to the developer.

If you use Android's backup feature, Android may include these settings in your
device backup with Google. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account,
not by the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes the settings from your phone.

## Links to other apps and websites

Tapping a phone number opens your phone app with the number filled in. It does not
place the call. Tapping a website link (for example TSA, FAA, the airport's lost and
found form or the Fly EUG site) opens it in your browser. Those websites and apps have
their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this app.

## Children

The app is a general travel guide and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly
collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date
at the top will change.
