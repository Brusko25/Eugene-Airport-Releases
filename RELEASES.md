## Withdrawn builds

0.9.0, the version-only 0.9.1 comparison, and the 0.9.2 dependency comparison were
briefly published on 2026-09-14, then withdrawn after Play Protect blocked installation
on the physical test phone. No downloads of those builds are offered while the cause
is investigated. Emulator compatibility checks did not predict the phone result.

0.9.3 is the latest comparison candidate, with its phone test pending. The last verified release is 0.8.1. Its normal in-app update passed Play Protect on the
physical Pixel 8a, retained settings and showed What's new. The 0.8.0 APK remains
available unchanged.
